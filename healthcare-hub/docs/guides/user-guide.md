# User guide

Healthcare Hub shows you only what your role needs. Every status has words and an icon as well as
a colour:

| Status  | Meaning                     |
| ------- | --------------------------- |
| ✓ Green | Current                     |
| ! Amber | Due soon or awaiting review |
| ✕ Red   | Overdue or blocked          |
| – Grey  | Not verified yet            |

**Uploading a document never makes anything green.** Someone authorised must verify it first.

## Registered manager

- **Home dashboard.** Overdue items, items due in 30 days, blocked vehicles and today's jobs. Each
  tile opens exactly the items it counts.
- **Reports.** Pick a report, a period and a location. Every figure says what it counts and out of
  what. Use Export CSV for the detail, and the Monthly management pack for the board.

## HR and training

Staff → a profile:

1. Move the person through checks and approve their roles.
2. Add records such as references, DBS, right to work, licence and training.
3. Verify uploaded evidence. You cannot verify your own upload.

The staff list shows each person's overall readiness. Open a profile to see exactly which requirement is missing.

## Fleet

Vehicles and assets:

- Each vehicle shows its status, inspections, defects, maintenance and expenses.
- **A critical or stop-use defect blocks the vehicle and sends its upcoming jobs back for
  reassignment.**
- Defects tab, step by step:
  1. **Classify** a minor defect with a repair deadline. You can also make it stop-use.
  2. **Start repair**, then **Sign off repair** and say what was repaired. Signing off does not
     put the vehicle back into service.
  3. Someone else with return-to-service rights checks the repair. They then either choose
     **Return to service** and record what they checked, or choose **Send back for repair** and
     say what is still wrong.
- Each defect shows who reported it, who signed off the repair and who returned it to service.
  Repair deadlines show as due soon or overdue. "Show: Returned to service" lists closed defects.

## Dispatch (Work planner)

1. Book journeys. Recurring journeys can be changed one at a time or "this and following".
2. Choose a shift for each job and **Validate**. Every check shows PASS, FAIL or PENDING with the
   reason.
3. **Allocate and notify crew** stays disabled while any check fails. Only competence gaps can have
   an approved, time-limited exception.
4. Late or unacknowledged offers raise an alert.

## Governance

Compliance:

- **Audits.**
  1. A template version is published by someone other than its author, then activated by
     management (again not the author). "View controls" shows the questions.
  2. Schedule an audit from an active template, naming the auditor, the owner and the due date.
  3. In the audit, record the sample (pick the staff, vehicles, equipment or locations by name)
     and a response for each control. Partial, non-compliant and not-applicable answers need a
     note.
  4. Submit for review. Responses are then fixed and a finding is worked out for each control.
  5. Raise an action or a re-audit from each failed finding, or add an observation the questions
     did not cover. **An action closes only when someone else verifies its evidence.**
- **Policies.** Add a policy with its owner and the roles that must read it. Each version goes
  draft → review → approved (by someone other than the author) → published. A material change
  means everyone acknowledges again; a minor one carries acknowledgements forward. "Details" shows
  the history and who still has to read it. Staff use "Read and acknowledge"; critical
  instructions need an explicit "read and understood" tick.
- **Events and complaints.** Each event moves step by step through triage, protect, assess,
  investigate, respond, actions and learning. Reporting duties and duty of candour are recorded.

Premises safety lists the checks for each location.

## Crew app

1. **Sign in** with your work account, then unlock with your fingerprint, face or PIN.
2. **My shift** shows your vehicle, crew and three cards: Driving clearance, Vehicle daily
   inspection and Pre-shift cleaning. You can start the shift only when all three are complete.
3. **Vehicle check.** Answer every item. A failed item needs a reason; add a photo if you can. A
   failed critical item stops the vehicle, and control is told.
4. **Jobs.** Read the access and handover notes before moving the patient. Use the large buttons
   only when stationary. Patient details are shown during your shift only.
5. **Problems.** Use Report defect. If the vehicle becomes unsafe during a journey, the app tells
   you to contact control now. Stay with your patient.
6. **No signal?** Keep working. Items show "pending sync" and send themselves when you are back
   online. Accepting a new job, or starting a shift without a recent check, needs control:
   **follow the downtime procedure and call control.**
